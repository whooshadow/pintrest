# Complete Beginner Guide: From Zero to Your First Pins
## (Images, Canva, Amazon links, Publishing — every step)

You don't need any experience. Follow this top to bottom.

====================================================================
SECTION A — WHERE TO GET IMAGES (100% free + legal)
====================================================================

You CANNOT just copy images off Google — many are copyrighted and can get
your account in trouble. Use these free, legal sources instead. All free,
no payment, allowed for commercial use:

1. CANVA'S OWN LIBRARY (easiest — built into the design tool)
   - When you design in Canva, it has millions of free photos/graphics
     built in. You often never need an outside image at all.

2. FREE STOCK PHOTO SITES (if you want specific photos):
   - unsplash.com
   - pexels.com
   - pixabay.com
   All three: free, no login needed, safe to use commercially.
   Search terms to use there: "zero waste", "reusable", "eco kitchen",
   "glass jars", "bamboo", "sustainable home".

3. AMAZON PRODUCT IMAGES — special rule:
   - As an Amazon Associate you ARE allowed to show product images,
     but ONLY if you pull them through Amazon's own tools (SiteStripe
     or product links). Don't screenshot them manually.
   - Easiest for beginners: don't bother with product photos at first.
     Use a nice "lifestyle" photo (jars, bamboo brushes) from Canva/Unsplash
     as your pin image, and put the Amazon link as the destination.

RULE OF THUMB: For your pin image, use a pretty lifestyle photo from Canva
or Unsplash. The actual product is what they see AFTER they click, on Amazon.

====================================================================
SECTION B — MAKE YOUR PIN IMAGE IN CANVA (click by click)
====================================================================

1. Go to canva.com -> "Sign up" -> use Google or email (FREE, pick free plan).

2. In the top search box, type:  Pinterest Pin
   -> Click it. This opens the correct size automatically (1000 x 1500).

3. On the LEFT you'll see templates. Pick any clean one you like.
   (Look for ones with a photo on top and a text bar — perfect for
   "10 Zero Waste Kitchen Swaps".)

4. CHANGE THE PHOTO:
   - Click the photo in the template.
   - On the left, click "Photos" (or "Elements" -> Photos).
   - Search "zero waste" or "glass jars kitchen".
   - Drag a photo you like ONTO the existing photo to replace it.

5. CHANGE THE TEXT:
   - Double-click the title text.
   - Delete it, type your title, e.g.:  10 Zero Waste Kitchen Swaps Under $15
   - You can change font/color on the top toolbar if you want.

6. (Optional) Add your Pinterest name small at the bottom for branding.

7. DOWNLOAD:
   - Top right: "Share" -> "Download".
   - File type: PNG.
   - Click "Download". The image saves to your computer (Downloads folder).

Repeat steps 2–7 for each pin. TIP: once you make one you like, click
"..." -> "Make a copy" and just change the text + photo for the next pin.
Much faster.

====================================================================
SECTION C — GET YOUR AMAZON LINK (the thing that earns money)
====================================================================

1. Make sure you're LOGGED IN to Amazon with your Associates account.

2. Go to amazon.com. At the very TOP you should see a black/grey bar =
   "SiteStripe". (If you don't see it: you're not logged in as an
   Associate, or the account isn't active yet.)

3. Search Amazon for a product, e.g. "beeswax wraps" or "reusable produce bags".
   Open the product page.

4. In the SiteStripe bar at the top, click:  "Get Link"  ->  "Text".

5. It shows a short link like  https://amzn.to/xxxxx
   -> Click "Copy". THIS is your money link (your ID is inside it).

6. Paste that link somewhere safe (a notes file) labeled with the product.
   You'll put it into the matching pin.

Do this for 3 products to start. Pick cheap, popular, useful eco items —
they sell more easily than expensive ones.

====================================================================
SECTION D — PUBLISH THE PIN ON PINTEREST (click by click)
====================================================================

1. Log into Pinterest (Business account).

2. Top-left: click "Create" -> "Create Pin".

3. UPLOAD: drag in your Canva PNG image (or click to browse to it).

4. TITLE box: paste your pin title.
   e.g.  10 Zero Waste Kitchen Swaps Under $15

5. DESCRIPTION box: paste your description WITH #ad and keywords.
   e.g.  Easy affordable zero waste kitchen swaps for beginners. Simple eco
   friendly ideas to cut plastic and save money. #ad #zerowaste #ecofriendly

6. "Add a destination link" box: paste your Amazon SiteStripe link.

7. Choose a board (e.g. "Zero Waste Kitchen Swaps").

8. Click "Publish".

CONGRATS — that pin can now earn money if someone clicks + buys.

====================================================================
SECTION E — THE DISCLOSURE RULE (don't skip)
====================================================================

EVERY pin that links to Amazon MUST say it's an affiliate/ad link.
- Put "#ad" in the description (shown in examples above). Required by
  Amazon AND Swedish marketing law. Takes 1 second, keeps you safe.

====================================================================
SECTION F — YOUR DAILY ROUTINE
====================================================================

Each day, ~20–30 min:
- Make 2–3 new pin images in Canva (copy an old one, swap text/photo).
- Reuse the Amazon links you already have, or grab new ones.
- Publish them, spaced out across the day if you can.
- Once a week: open Pinterest Analytics, see which pins got the most
  clicks, and make more pins like those.

DON'T expect sales day 1. Pinterest shows your pins to more people over
weeks. Impressions + clicks first, sales usually month 2–3. This is normal.
