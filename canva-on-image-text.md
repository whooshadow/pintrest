# Canva On-Image Text — 10 Pins

Last updated: 2026-10-26

This is the TEXT THAT GOES ON THE IMAGE itself (the big headline people see
in their feed). It's different from the Pinterest title/description (those are
in pins-ready-to-paste.md and go in the text fields when you upload).

HOW TO USE in Canva:
1. Open your Pinterest Pin template (1000 x 1500 px).
2. Replace the headline text with the "MAIN TEXT" below.
3. Optional small line = "SUBTEXT".
4. Add your handle "@ordinarydayinyourlife" small at the bottom (branding).
5. Keep text BIG and high-contrast — most views are on a phone.
6. Share -> Download -> PNG.

TIP: make ONE good template, then "Make a copy" and just swap the text +
background photo for each. Use free photos from Canva / Unsplash / Pexels.
