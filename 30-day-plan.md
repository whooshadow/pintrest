# 30-Day Pinterest Affiliate Test Plan
## Niche: Zero-Waste / Sustainable Home Swaps on a Budget

**Goal:** Test whether this niche drives clicks + sales, using 100% free tools.
**Why this niche:** Fast-growing (2026), real search traffic, beatable competition,
high buyer intent, works anonymous, sells BOTH physical products AND high-margin
digital products.

---

## PART 1: KEYWORDS (verified from live Pinterest search data)

Pinterest is a SEARCH engine. Put these words in your pin titles, descriptions,
board names, and profile. These are real, actively-searched terms:

### Primary keywords (high volume, use often)
- zero waste swaps
- eco friendly living for beginners
- zero waste on a budget
- plastic free living
- sustainable living for beginners
- low waste kitchen
- eco friendly swaps
- zero waste home

### Long-tail keywords (lower competition, high intent — easier to rank)
- zero waste kitchen swaps on a budget
- affordable eco swaps for beginners
- plastic free home on a budget
- zero waste bathroom essentials
- reusable kitchen products
- cheap sustainable swaps
- zero waste swaps checklist

### Buyer-intent phrases (people about to spend money)
- "best reusable [X]"
- "plastic free alternatives to [X]"
- "eco friendly [product] worth it"

---

## PART 2: AFFILIATE PROGRAMS (all free to join)

### Physical products — eco/zero-waste stores
Typical commission for this niche (from research):
- Reusable bottles/containers: 10–25%
- Zero-waste personal care: 20–35%  <- highest physical %
- Eco home goods: 12–20%
- Sustainable fashion: 15–30%

Programs to apply to (free):
- **EarthHero** — large eco marketplace, good for home/kitchen
- **Zero Waste Store** — dedicated zero-waste marketplace
- **Greenzla / Mioeco / Georganics** — reusable + personal care (high %)
- **Biome Eco Stores** — broad eco range (via Commission Factory)
- **Amazon Associates** — backup for "eco finds" (low % 1–4% but huge selection,
  great for learning what converts)

Networks that host many of these (sign up free, then browse advertisers):
- Awin, ShareASale, Impact, FlexOffers
- Adtraction / Tradedoubler (Nordic/Sweden-friendly)

### Digital products — HIGH commission (30–50%), least ongoing work
- Sustainable living e-courses / guides (ClickBank, Digistore24)
- OR create your OWN printable (see Part 4) = you keep ~95%

RULE: Confirm each program allows Pinterest traffic before relying on it.
Most do; some require you to link to your own page first.

---

## PART 3: FREE SETUP (Day 1–2)

1. Create a free **Pinterest Business account** (pinterest.com/business).
   - Username + display name should include a keyword,
     e.g. "Budget Zero Waste Home".
   - Bio: use 2–3 primary keywords naturally.
2. Create 5 boards, each named with keywords:
   - "Zero Waste Kitchen Swaps"
   - "Plastic Free Home on a Budget"
   - "Eco Friendly Swaps for Beginners"
   - "Zero Waste Bathroom"
   - "Sustainable Living Tips"
3. Set up a free landing layer (improves conversion + keeps programs happy):
   - Option A: free "link in bio" page (Beacons / Linktree free tier)
   - Option B: free blog (WordPress.com / Medium) with simple product roundup posts
4. Design tool: **Canva free**. Pin size = 1000 x 1500 px (2:3 vertical).
5. Apply to 2–3 affiliate programs from Part 2.

## PART 4: 10 READY-TO-USE PIN IDEAS

Each should be a vertical image + keyword title + keyword description + link.

1. "10 Zero Waste Kitchen Swaps Under $15" -> links to product roundup
2. "Plastic Free Bathroom on a Budget (Beginner Guide)"
3. "Reusable Products That Actually Save You Money"
4. "Zero Waste Swaps Checklist (Free Printable)" <- lead magnet, builds email list
5. "7 Eco Swaps I Wish I'd Made Sooner"
6. "Budget Zero Waste Starter Kit (Everything Under $50)"
7. "Plastic Free Alternatives to Everyday Kitchen Items"
8. "How to Start Zero Waste Without Spending a Fortune"
9. "Beeswax Wraps vs Plastic Wrap — Worth It?" (comparison = high intent)
10. "Zero Waste Cleaning Swaps That Actually Work"

TIP: Pin #4 (free printable) is your secret weapon — it's a DIGITAL product you
make once in Canva, give away to build an email list, then sell an upgraded
version. Highest margin, most passive.

---

## PART 5: 30-DAY POSTING SCHEDULE

### Week 1 — Setup + learn
- Days 1–2: Account, boards, join programs, make landing page
- Days 3–7: Create + publish 3 pins/day (mix from the 10 ideas above).
  Test different styles/titles. GOAL = learn what gets clicks.

### Week 2 — Volume + observe
- 3–5 pins/day. Reuse winning formats with new titles/images.
- Check Pinterest Analytics: which pins get impressions + clicks?

### Week 3 — Double down
- Make MORE pins like your top 3 performers.
- Add affiliate links to your best-traffic pins.
- Push the free-printable pin harder (list building).

### Week 4 — Optimize + decide
- Scale winners, drop losers.
- Review: clicks? sign-ups? any sales?
- Decide: lean more into physical products or digital/printables?

---

## PART 6: RULES (don't get banned)

- ALWAYS disclose affiliate links: "#ad" / "affiliate link" on pin or landing page
  (required by Pinterest + Swedish marketing law / consumer protection).
- No misleading claims.
- Read Pinterest's current affiliate policy (it changes).
- Don't spam identical pins — vary images and titles.

---

## SUCCESS METRICS FOR THE TEST (end of 30 days)

- Impressions growing week over week? (yes = SEO working)
- Outbound click-through happening? (yes = pins are compelling)
- Any email sign-ups from the free printable? (yes = list building works)
- Any sales? (bonus in month 1 — real money usually starts month 2–3 as pins mature)

Pinterest pins take weeks to gain traction, so judge month 1 on IMPRESSIONS +
CLICKS, not just sales.
